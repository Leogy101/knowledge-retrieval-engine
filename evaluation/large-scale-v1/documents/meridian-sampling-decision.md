# Meridian ratified sampling decision

Synthetic Meridian/Harbor evaluation document. No real organization, secret or person is represented.

Decision M-27 sets the coastal station sampling interval to sixty seconds during ordinary operation. Only a signed incident directive may shorten it to ten seconds.

M-27 is the ratified field-governance decision. The experimental dashboard and unsigned field notes cannot change station duty cycles.

The incident directive identifies its incident, affected stations and expiry; a graph showing a higher rate is not a directive.
