# Meridian operator receipt handbook

Synthetic Meridian/Harbor evaluation document. No real organization, secret or person is represented.

Operators check /receipts/{receipt_id} for the publication state. The accepted, review_pending, published and rejected states are distinct.

A support ticket for a stalled receipt includes receipt_id, station_id and the last observed state. It must not include a private raw sensor attachment.

The public observation listing is not a reliable admission receipt: unpublished items do not appear there.
