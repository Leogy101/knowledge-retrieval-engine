# Meridian ratified uplink decision

Synthetic Meridian/Harbor evaluation document. No real organization, secret or person is represented.

Ratified decision M-31 requires satellite uplink batches of at most twelve envelopes. An operator may split a batch; the operator may not raise the maximum.

Only a replacement ratified M-series decision can alter the twelve-envelope satellite maximum. Vendor capability sheets describe hardware potential, not an approved fleet configuration.

The terrestrial gateway has a different limit; a terrestrial example does not amend satellite policy.
