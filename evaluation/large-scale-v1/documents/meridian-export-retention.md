# Meridian export expiration policy

Synthetic Meridian/Harbor evaluation document. No real organization, secret or person is represented.

Research export bundles expire after seven days. At expiry the download link is disabled; the audit manifest remains for one hundred eighty days.

Disabling an export link does not erase copies that a recipient already downloaded. Recall notices name the immutable bundle identifier.

Audit retention and bundle availability are intentionally different lifetimes.
