# Meridian observation publication policy

Synthetic Meridian/Harbor evaluation document. No real organization, secret or person is represented.

A queued observation becomes publishable only after calibration provenance and station consent both validate. Publication is asynchronous and can lag admission.

If either calibration provenance or consent is unresolved, retain the observation in the review queue. Queue acceptance does not waive either requirement.

Reviewers inspect the immutable receipt identifier when tracing an observation from upload to public release.
